## 🧠 What I'm Currently Exploring

### General Relativity

I'm interested in understanding gravity geometrically, including:

[
ds^2 = g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu
]

and how the metric determines geodesics, curvature and observable phenomena.

Some of the problems I'm interested in exploring computationally include:

* photon trajectories around black holes
* gravitational lensing
* Schwarzschild geodesics
* gravitational redshift
* orbital dynamics
* visualisations of curved spacetime

### Quantum Physics

I'm interested in the mathematical structure of quantum mechanics, particularly:

[
i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t}
=====================================

\hat{H}\psi
]

and the connections between linear algebra, differential equations and physical observables.

### Mathematics

I'm particularly interested in the mathematics that underpins theoretical physics:

**Linear Algebra → Differential Equations → Analysis → Geometry → Mathematical Physics**

I enjoy taking physical problems and trying to understand what mathematical structure is actually responsible for the result.
